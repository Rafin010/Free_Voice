How it works

1

Install normally

Run FreeVoice-Setup.exe and complete the standard Windows setup wizard. Launch FreeVoice from your desktop or Start menu.

2

Press ` once

Place your cursor in any app, such as Chrome, VS Code, Word, or Notepad, and press ` once to start Listening....

3

Speak & press ` again

Speak naturally in English or Bengali. Press ` a second time to transcribe and type your words directly at the cursor.

Shortcuts & Requirements

Action Shortcut / Detail
Voice typing (Toggle mode, default) Press ` once to start listening, press ` again to stop and insert text
Voice typing (Hold mode, optional) Switch in tray menu: hold ` to speak, release to insert text
Type a literal backtick (`) Press Ctrl + `
Type a literal tilde (~) Press Shift + ` (works normally)
